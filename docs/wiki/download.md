# Downloads

The complete A/B/C dataset archive is being prepared and verified. This page will list the `all`, `annotations`, and `clipped` archive parts, their sizes and SHA-256 checksums, and the Google Drive location after remote readback succeeds.

The three groups follow the split-archive distribution style of the earlier [ASD3DP record on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/21313911). The final ASD3DP-DG files have not yet been published here. Do not combine archive parts from different datasets.

The [GitHub repository](https://github.com/LUDO-Lab-Research/ASD3DP-DG) contains the public guide and research code references.
