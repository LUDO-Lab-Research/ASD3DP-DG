The complete A/B/C dataset archive is being prepared and verified. This page will list the all, annotations, and clipped archive parts, their sizes and SHA-256 checksums, and the Google Drive location after remote readback succeeds.

The three groups follow the split-archive distribution style of the earlier ASD3DP record on Zenodo. The final ASD3DP-DG files have not yet been published here. Do not combine archive parts from different datasets.

The GitHub repository contains the public guide and research code references.
